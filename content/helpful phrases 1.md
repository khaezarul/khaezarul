### Helpful phrases

How are you?

Ki saki sin?

---

I'm good.

No sorin.

---

I'm not good.

No sorin pa.

---

I'm bad.

No mapsan.

---

I'm good, just tired.

No sorin, sirsan dain.

---

I'm good, but tired.

No sorin, mokshë sirsan.

---

I'm bored.

No glaran.

---

I'm going to join call.

No la'ënsënë nongimin.

---

What do we play?

Mis saki hifora?

---

What are you doing?

Olt saki emimëra?

---

Good day!

Ngo sori!

---

Good morning!

Sarën sori!

---

Good afternoon!

Plen sori!

---

Good evening!

Seshpë sori!

---

Good night!

Tes sori!
