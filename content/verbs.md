### Conjugation

1sg (I/me) => (s)(e)-

2/3sg (you, he/she/they) => /

plural (we, you, they) => mar-

### Tenses

"P" stands for the person conjugations above.

|         | imperfective | perfective | continuous  | habitual      |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| past    | -P-to        | -P-tsi     | CV- -P-to   | CVĸa- -P-to   |
| present | /            | -P-(e)n    | CV-P-       | CVĸa-P-       |
| future  | -P-gu        | -P-ban     | CV- -P-gu   | CVĸa- -P-gu   |

*-tsi*, *-gu* and *-ban* can change in the unmarked form (no -si- or -mar-). They can be "hard" (-tsi, -gu, -ban) or soft (-shi, -shu, -wan). Hard is assumed as the default. If the soft form is used, it is marked as "soft tense suffixes". In addition to this, *-tsi* becomes *-ti* after {s, sh}.

The soft form is also always used after the person form -si-, and the hard form after -mar-.

{e, o} becomes {i, u} before *-gu* and *-ban* (this can happen in soft forms as well).
