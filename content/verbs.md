### Conjugation
##### Personal markers

These are the suffixes that change according to who is the subject of the sentence. English "I see" vs "he see*s*" is an example of this.

The first person singular (I/me) gets the suffix -(ë)n.

The second and third person singular get the base form of the verb.

The plurals get the suffix -ra.

---
##### Tenses

These are the tense suffixes. "CV" stands for reduplication of the last consonant and vowel, detailed at [[reduplication]]. The personal markers come after the tense markers.

|         | imperfective | perfective | continuous | habitual |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------- | ---------- | -------- |
| past    | -ko          | -la        | -CVko      | -CVla    |
| present | /            | -da        | -CV        | -CVda    |
| future  | -mi          | -to        | -CVmi      | -CVto    |

Here are the english equivalents (with "do" used as the example verb), so you can see the meaning of these tenses:

|         | imperfective | perfective       | continuous      | habitual        |
| ------- | ------------ | ---------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| past    | I did        | I had done       | I was doing     | I used to do    |
| present | I do         | I have done      | I am doing      | I often do      |
| future  | I will do    | I will have done | I will be doing | I will often do |
